The Mission

Bridging the fatal gap between human limitation and immediate underwater mechanical intervention.

The Problem

Drowning remains one of the most critical causes of accidental fatalities. Unpredictable situations such as hypoxic blackouts, sudden exhaustion, or medical emergencies underwater frequently lead to fatal consequences.

Traditional safety protocols—like lifeguard surveillance—rely entirely on human observation, water clarity, and delayed reaction times. Once a diver loses consciousness, every second counts, and current wearable tech lacks autonomous actuation.

The HASA Innovation & Software

HASA is a fully self-sufficient wrist wearable that autonomously detects unconsciousness using an advanced biomedical SpO2 and BPM sensor. Upon detecting a critical crash in oxygen saturation, it instantly triggers a CO2-powered buoyant balloon, executing a controlled ascent to safety.

Software Integration: HASA doesn't just react; it analyzes. The device continuously streams real-time vitals to our highly secure Diver Dashboard, providing proactive risk assessments, mapping oxygen consumption curves, and issuing pre-crash fatigue warnings to athletes and trainers.

System Architecture

A flawless, multi-stage operational framework designed for absolute reliability.

01. Live Telemetry

Continuous monitoring of blood oxygen (SpO2) and heart rate (BPM) via the integrated MAX30102 sensor module.

02. Algorithmic Processing

The onboard microcontroller evaluates physiological data in real-time against pre-calibrated safety thresholds.

03. Critical Threshold Breach

If a hypoxic blackout is detected (SpO2 drops critically), the automated emergency protocol is instantly locked in.

04. Mechanical Actuation

A signal is sent to the solenoid valve, piercing the 16g CO2 cartridge and releasing compressed gas.

05. Rapid Ascension

The buoyant balloon fully inflates, lifting the incapacitated diver safely to the surface at a regulated, decompression-safe rate.
